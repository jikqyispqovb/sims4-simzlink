## Overview

Simzlink is a The Sims 4 mod that adds autonomous story progression to unplayed Sim households, allowing off-lot Sims to age naturally, form and end relationships, have children, change careers, and experience life events without the player needing to control them. This creates a living, breathing neighborhood that evolves alongside your active household.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **The Sims 4 Simzlink** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Autonomous Aging** | Unplayed Sims age through life stages without player intervention |
| **Relationship Events** | NPCs autonomously form, deepen, and end relationships over time |
| **Career Changes** | Background Sims change jobs based on personality traits and opportunities |
| **Family Growth** | Unplayed couples can autonomously have children and grow families |
